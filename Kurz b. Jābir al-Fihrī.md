# Kurz b. Jābir al-Fihrī (كرز بن جابر الفهري) ﵁
*A chief of the polytheists who raided the pasturing animals of Madīnah and later became Muslim; commander of the horsemen sent after the ʿUraniyyūn; killed on the day of the Conquest of Makkah*[^i5.435]

Kurz b. Jābir al-Qurashī al-Fihrī was one of the chiefs of the polytheists before he became Muslim. He raided the pasturing animals of Madīnah, and the Prophet ﷺ went out after him as far as Safawān; he then became Muslim, and the Prophet ﷺ appointed him commander over twenty horsemen sent after the men of ʿUraynah. He was killed on the day of the Conquest of Makkah.[^i5.434][^i5.435]

## Name and lineage

He was Kurz b. Jābir b. Ḥisl b. al-Ajab b. Ḥabīb b. ʿAmr b. Shaybān b. Muḥārib b. Fihr, of [[Quraysh]], al-Fihrī.[^i5.434]

## Before Islam

Kurz was one of the chiefs of the polytheists before he became Muslim. He once raided the *sarḥ* of Madīnah, and the Prophet ﷺ went out in pursuit of him until he reached Safawān; Kurz escaped him. This was the first expedition of Badr, and Kurz became Muslim afterwards.[^i5.434] The *sarḥ* used to pasture at al-Jammāʾ and its surroundings, and the Prophet ﷺ sought Kurz until he reached Safawān, on the side of Badr, without overtaking him (see [[Ghazwat Safawān]]).[^s4.16]

## Commander of the horsemen sent after the ʿUraniyyūn

When the ʿUraniyyūn attacked the Prophet's ﷺ servant and drove off the camels, the Prophet ﷺ sent horsemen of the Muslims after them with Kurz b. Jābir al-Fihrī as their commander. The chain of this report has a weak transmitter, Mūsā b. Muḥammad al-Taymī, but Yazīd b. Rūmān corroborated him.[^i5.434]

According to the report from Yazīd b. Rūmān, a group of eight men from ʿUraynah came and became Muslim, and found Madīnah unhealthy. When they had recovered and grown fat they attacked the milch camels and drove them off. Yasār, the freedman of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ, overtook them and fought them; they cut off his hand and foot and thrust thorns into his tongue and eyes, and he died.[^i5.434][^i5.435] When this reached the Prophet ﷺ he sent twenty horsemen in their tracks and appointed Kurz b. Jābir over them. They set out in the morning and met a woman carrying the shoulder of a camel; she told them she had passed a people who had slaughtered a camel and given her this, and that they were in that wilderness. The horsemen went, found them and took them captive.[^i5.435] (See [[Sariyyat al-ʿUraniyyīn]].)

## The Conquest of Makkah

Kurz was martyred on the day of the Conquest, among those who were with [[Khālid b. al-Walīd]], together with [[Ḥubaysh b. Khālid al-Khuzāʿī|Ḥubaysh b. Khālid]]. The two strayed from the army and took another road, and were killed.[^i5.435] He became Muslim and was martyred in the expedition of the Conquest.[^s4.16]

The Prophet ﷺ ordered Khālid b. al-Walīd to enter from the upper part of Makkah, and two men of Khālid's horse were killed that day, Ḥubaysh b. al-Ashʿar al-Khuzāʿī and Kurz b. Jābir al-Fihrī.[^i5.435]
