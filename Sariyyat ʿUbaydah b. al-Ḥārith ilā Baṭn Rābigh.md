# Sariyyat ʿUbaydah b. al-Ḥārith ilā Baṭn Rābigh (سرية عبيدة بن الحارث إلى بطن رابغ)
*The expedition of ʿUbaydah b. al-Ḥārith to Baṭn Rābigh, in which the first arrow in Islam was shot*

In Shawwāl of the first year, [[ʿUbaydah b. al-Ḥārith]] ﵁ went out with sixty or eighty riders of the [[al-Muhājirūn]], none of them an Anṣārī, and met [[Abū Sufyān b. Ḥarb]] with two hundred at a water in Baṭn Rābigh. There was shooting between the two parties, in which [[Saʿd b. Abī Waqqāṣ]] ﵁ shot the first arrow shot in Islam; then they withdrew.[^s6.13]

## The force

ʿUbaydah's banner was white, and Misṭaḥ b. Uthāthah b. ʿAbbād b. al-Muṭṭalib b. ʿAbd Manāf ﵁ carried it.[^s6.13] ʿUbaydah went out and met Abū Sufyān at a water called Aḥyāʾ, of Baṭn Rābigh, about ten miles from [[al-Juḥfah]].[^s6.13] Aḥyāʾ is a water below Thaniyyat al-Marrah.[^s6.13] The polytheists had turned off the road to pasture their riding beasts.[^s6.13]

## The shooting

The two parties shot at one another. They drew no sword and did not draw up in ranks; there was only skirmishing (*munāwashah*), the two sides coming close and seizing at one another.[^s6.13][^s6.14] Saʿd shot an arrow in the path of Allah, and it was the first arrow shot in Islam. He emptied out his quiver, which held twenty arrows, and went forward in front of his companions, who shielded him; there was not one of the arrows but wounded a man or an animal.[^s6.13] Nothing else passed between them that day, and the two parties went away with their rearguards.[^s6.13][^s6.14]

## Al-Miqdād and ʿUtbah

Two men fled from the unbelievers to the Muslims: [[al-Miqdād b. ʿAmr]] al-Bahrānī, an ally of [[Banū Zuhrah]], and [[ʿUtbah b. Ghazwān]] al-Māzinī, an ally of Banū Nawfal b. ʿAbd Manāf. They were Muslims, but had gone out with the polytheists in order to get through.[^s6.13]

## Disputes

**The commander of the polytheists.** Abū Sufyān was over the polytheists; Muḥammad b. ʿUmar says this is the sound view with him. It is also said to have been Mikraz b. Ḥafṣ, and it is said [[ʿIkrimah b. Abī Jahl]].[^s6.13] It is not known that Mikraz became a Muslim; Ibn Ḥibbān alone mentions him among the Ṣaḥābah, saying "it is said he had companionship", and if that is sound he became a Muslim, and if not, not.[^s6.14]

**The number of riders.** The chapter heading gives sixty or eighty.[^s6.13]

**The year.** More than one of the biographers mention this expedition in the first year. Abū al-Aswad in his *Maghāzī*, connected by Ibn ʿĀʾidh from Ibn ʿAbbās, relates that when the Prophet ﷺ reached [[Ghazwat al-Abwāʾ|al-Abwāʾ]] he sent ʿUbaydah b. al-Ḥārith with sixty men; that would place it in the second year, and some of the biographers say so explicitly. Allah knows best.[^s6.13]
